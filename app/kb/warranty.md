# Warranty Policy

## Coverage
All products carry a 12-month limited warranty against manufacturing defects, starting at the delivery date.

## Claims after the 30-day return window
Defects reported after 30 days but within the 12-month warranty are handled as warranty claims: repair or replacement, not a refund. These must be escalated to a human warranty specialist.

## Exclusions
Warranty does not cover accidental damage, water damage, or unauthorized modification.

# Refund Policy

## Return window
Customers may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

## Defective products
Items that arrive defective or stop working within 30 days of delivery are eligible for a full refund or replacement. No return shipping fee applies for defective items.

## Duplicate or incorrect charges
If a customer was charged twice or billed an incorrect amount, the extra amount is refunded in full once the duplicate charge is verified against the order record.

## Refund approval limits
Refunds of 50 USD or less may be issued automatically by the support agent. Refunds above 50 USD, and any refund on a high-urgency case, require approval from a human support lead before the money is released.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5-7 business days.

# Shipping Times and Delivery Issues

## Standard and express delivery
Standard shipping takes 3-7 business days. Express shipping takes 1-2 business days. Tracking numbers are emailed when an order ships.

## Late deliveries
If an order is more than 7 days past its estimated delivery date, it is treated as delayed or lost. The customer is eligible for a full refund of the order total or a free reshipment.

## Orders in transit
For orders still within the estimated delivery window, share the current tracking status and expected delivery date. No refund is issued until the window has passed.

## Delivered but not received
If an order shows as delivered but the customer did not receive it, escalate to a human agent to open a carrier investigation.
